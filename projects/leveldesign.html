Level Design

A collection of my level design documents, focusing on structure, pacing, gameplay flow, and player experience. Each LDD explores a different approach to planning and communicating level ideas clearly and professionally.

LDD 01 — Level Design Document

This document outlines the core structure, goals, and gameplay flow for the first level concept. It includes layout planning, player progression, challenge pacing, and environmental storytelling elements. The focus of this LDD is clarity and readability, ensuring the level can be understood and built by a full development team.

LDD 02 — Level Design Document

The second LDD expands on more advanced level design ideas, including multi‑path layouts, interactive elements, and refined gameplay beats. This document explores how player choice, difficulty curves, and environmental cues can shape a more dynamic and engaging level experience.

Takeaway

These documents represent my approach to level design: clear communication, intentional pacing, and a strong focus on player experience. Each LDD is built to be readable, actionable, and ready for implementation in a production environment.
