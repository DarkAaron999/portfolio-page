Medieval Fantasy Mace

Hand‑painted 3D weapon inspired by high‑fantasy RPGs

This mace was created as a hero prop for a fantasy RPG. The goal was to develop a clean, readable silhouette supported by a hand‑painted texture style and a strong magical identity. The model was retopologized in Maya and textured in Substance Painter.
