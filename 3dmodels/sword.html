Medieval Fantasy Sword

Hand‑painted hero prop for a high‑fantasy RPG

This 3D sword was created as a hero prop for a fantasy RPG, with a focus on a clean, readable silhouette and hand-painted texture style. The model was retopologized in Maya and textured in Substance Painter.
