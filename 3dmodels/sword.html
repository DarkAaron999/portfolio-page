Medieval Fantasy Sword

Hand‑crafted 3D weapon inspired by high‑fantasy RPGs

This sword was designed as a hero prop for a fantasy RPG. The goal was to create a clean, readable silhouette with stylized materials and a strong magical identity. The model was sculpted in ZBrush, retopologized in Blender, and textured in Substance Painter.
