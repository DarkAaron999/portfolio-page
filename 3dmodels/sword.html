Medieval Fantasy Sword

Hand-painted fantasy sword model

Use your mouse or touch to orbit, pan, and zoom the model.

This sword was modeled in Maya and textured in Substance Painter.

Process

This section documents the full workflow behind creating the Medieval Fantasy Sword, from early references to final textures. Each stage highlights the decisions, iterations, and techniques used throughout the project.

References

A collection of visual inspiration and design references used to guide the silhouette, proportions, and overall fantasy style of the sword.

Turnarounds

Early turnaround sketches and orthographic views used to establish the sword’s shape before moving into 3D modeling.

Low Poly, High Poly & UV Layout

The modeling phase included creating a clean low‑poly mesh, refining details in a high‑poly sculpt, and preparing the UV layout for hand‑painted texturing.

Trial and Error Textures

Iterations created in Substance Painter while experimenting with color palettes, brush styles, roughness values, and hand‑painted detailing.

Final Texture

The completed hand‑painted texture map, polished for clarity, contrast, and stylized fantasy appeal.
