Medieval Fantasy Sword

Hand‑painted hero prop for a high‑fantasy RPG

This sword was created as a hero prop for a fantasy RPG. The goal was to develop a clean, readable silhouette supported by a hand‑painted texture style and a strong magical identity. The model was retopologized in Maya and textured in Substance Painter.

Project Info

  • Role: 3D Artist
  • Software: Maya, Substance Painter, Photoshop
  • Workflow: Low‑poly → High‑poly → UV-unwrapping → Texture

Modeling Workflow

The high‑poly sculpt established the main shapes, bevels, and magical silhouette.

The low‑poly mesh was optimized for real‑time use while preserving the silhouette.

UVs were laid out to maximize texture resolution and minimize distortion.

Turnarounds helped refine proportions and ensure readability from all angles.
