Design Week 01 — Guided Exploration in Minecraft

A collaborative level built in Minecraft: Education Edition, designed to guide players through a curated exploration path using poetry, lighting cues, and environmental storytelling.

Design Challenge

The goal for this week was to create an environment in Minecraft: Education Edition that intentionally guided players through a structured exploration experience. Our team integrated poetry, environmental storytelling, and level design principles to craft a journey that felt both intuitive and emotionally grounded.

Key Lessons

  • Collaboration & Alignment. Our initial ideas varied widely, but through discussion and compromise we established a shared vision that balanced creativity with clarity.
  • Environmental Storytelling. Lighting, structure, and spatial pacing became our primary tools for guiding players without explicit instructions.
  • Iterative Playtesting. Each playtest revealed friction points in path clarity, pacing, and player orientation. Small adjustments—like shifting light sources or reshaping pathways—had a big impact.

Process

  • Poetry Integration. Each poem influenced the emotional tone and thematic flow of its corresponding area.
  • Sketching. I explored layout ideas through quick 2D sketches before building in Minecraft.
  • Greyboxing. Our team divided the level into sections; I focused on the enclosure area, shaping its pacing and spatial framing.
  • Lighting & Cues. We used lighting, elevation, and contrast to subtly guide players toward key points of interest.

Takeaway

Designing for guided exploration requires a balance of subtlety and clarity. Through iteration, we learned how lighting, pacing, and spatial framing can gently lead players without breaking immersion. Working in Minecraft: Education Edition also reinforced how powerful simple tools can be when paired with strong design intent.
