Design Week 01 — Guided Exploration in Minecraft

A collaborative Minecraft level designed to guide players through an intentional exploration path using poetry, lighting, and environmental storytelling.

Design Challenge: Create a Minecraft environment that intentionally guides players through an exploration experience, integrating a curated section of poems. The work combined level design, sketching, and playtesting in a collaborative setting.

Key Lessons

  • Collaboration & Communication. Our team began with differing ideas about visual style and how to integrate poetry. Through discussion and compromise, we aligned on a shared vision balancing creativity with functionality. Clear roles and responsibilities kept us organized and ensured meaningful contributions across the board.

Environmental Storytelling

  • Lighting — Used to create breadcrumbs for the path and catch the player’s attention.
  • Section Breaking — Dividing the level into distinct thematic areas.

Playtesting & Iteration

  • Path clarity — Simplified pathing and refined lighting cues.
  • Section pacing — Adjusted the flow and rhythm of each area.
  • Player spawn — Ensured players begin oriented and facing the intended direction.

Process Notes

  • Poetry Integration. Poems were used to shape the emotional tone and narrative of the level.
  • Sketching. I began with quick 2D sketches to explore layout ideas.
  • Greyboxing. Our team divided the level into sections; I focused on the enclosure area.
  • Environmental Storytelling. Used environmental cues to reinforce the poem’s themes.

Takeaway

Guiding players effectively in a freeform space like Minecraft hinges on wayfinding, pacing, and player orientation, all validated through playtesting. Iterating on small friction points made the biggest difference in player confidence and engagement.
