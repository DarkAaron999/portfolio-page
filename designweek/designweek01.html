Design Week 01 — Guided Exploration in Minecraft

A collaborative Minecraft level built to guide players through a curated exploration path using poetry, lighting cues, and environmental storytelling.

Design Challenge: Create a Minecraft environment that intentionally guides players through a curated exploration experience while integrating a selection of poems. Our team combined level design, sketching, and playtesting to shape a cohesive journey.

Key Lessons

  • Collaboration & Alignment. Early ideas varied widely, but through discussion and compromise we established a shared vision that balanced creativity with clarity.
  • Environmental Storytelling. Lighting, structure, and spatial pacing became our primary tools for guiding players.
  • Iterative Playtesting. Each test revealed friction points in path clarity, pacing, and player orientation, which we refined through small but meaningful adjustments.

Process

  • Poetry Integration. Poems shaped the emotional tone and thematic flow of each section.
  • Sketching. I explored layout ideas through quick 2D sketches before building.
  • Greyboxing. Our team divided the level into sections; I focused on the enclosure area.
  • Lighting & Cues. Used to subtly guide players without explicit instructions.

Takeaway

Designing for guided exploration requires a balance of subtlety and clarity. Through iteration, we learned how lighting, pacing, and spatial framing can gently lead players without breaking immersion.
