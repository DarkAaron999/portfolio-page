Design Week 02 — Micro Mix Game

A fast, arcade‑style micro game built around a single verb, developed through rapid iteration, sketching, and a console‑style compliancy check.

Design Challenge: Build a micro‑mix arcade game centered entirely around one verb. Before choosing the verb, our team explored multiple concepts. One idea I pitched was Stew of the Day — a timing‑based cooking game where ingredients fall toward a pot and must be sliced mid‑air.

After comparing concepts, we selected the verb fight, which led us toward a fighting rhythm game where timing and impact defined the experience.

My primary responsibility was creating the game’s background. Even though gameplay was 2D, I built the environment in 3D to give us more control over lighting, depth, and atmosphere. This approach helped the visuals feel dynamic while supporting gameplay clarity.

The project also included a compliancy check modeled after console certification. This required stable performance, readable UI, consistent controls, and no soft‑locks — a valuable exercise in polish and discipline.

Key Lessons

  • Pitching & Ideation. Exploring multiple concepts clarified what makes a strong single‑verb game.
  • Verb‑Driven Design. Choosing the verb after exploring ideas created a more natural design flow.
  • Compliancy Thinking. Treating the project like a console submission improved our attention to detail.
  • Scope Awareness. Micro games demand clarity — every element must support the core verb.

Visual Direction

  • 3D‑to‑2D Workflow. Built a 3D environment to support a 2D gameplay loop.
  • Lighting Control. 3D lighting helped reinforce rhythm and impact.
  • Supporting the Verb. The background enhanced the feel of “fight” without distracting from gameplay.

Takeaway

This project reinforced how much clarity and intention matter when designing around a single verb. Iteration — from pitching ideas to refining sketches to polishing the final environment — shaped the experience. The compliancy check added a layer of professional discipline that elevated the final result.
