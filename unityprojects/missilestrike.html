Missile Strike

A fast‑reaction defense game where you launch and steer a missile to intercept enemies before they reach the silo.

Overview

Missile Strike is a reaction‑based defense game built in Unity. Your goal is to launch a missile from the silo and manually guide it to destroy incoming enemies. I created all of the code and art assets for this project, including the missile, UI, effects, and gameplay logic.

How to Play

  • Release the Missile: Click the missile button to launch it from the silo.
  • Control the Missile: Click and drag your mouse over the missile to steer it in real time.
  • Objective: Destroy enemies before they reach the silo. Survive as long as possible.

Play the WebGL Build

The game is fully playable in your browser through the embedded itch.io WebGL build below.

Development Notes

This project helped me explore real‑time input control, projectile steering, and timing‑based gameplay. Creating both the art and code allowed me to design visuals that support the fast‑paced mechanics.
