About Me

Game Designer • Technical Artist • 2D/3D Generalist

I’m a third-year student in the Bachelor of Game Design program at Sheridan College, specializing in Unity development, technical art, and 2D/3D asset creation. Over the past four years, I’ve built a wide range of projects — from digital games and hybrid prototypes to board games and progression systems — both independently and in collaborative team settings.

My Background

My work blends design thinking with hands-on production. At Sheridan, I’ve developed strong skills in Unity, C#, 3D modeling, texturing, 2D art, and collaborative workflows. I enjoy taking ideas from concept to polished prototype, whether that means building systems, creating assets, or refining player experience.

What I Do

  • 3D modeling, sculpting, and texturing
  • 2D art, icons, UI assets, and visual polish
  • Unity development and gameplay programming
  • Level design, worldbuilding, and environment storytelling
  • Rapid prototyping and iterative design

My Approach

I focus on clarity, readability, and player experience. Whether I’m designing a mechanic, building a level, or creating art, I aim for work that feels intentional, cohesive, and easy to understand at a glance. I enjoy solving problems, refining workflows, and making systems feel smooth and intuitive.
